"Split evenly on a floor" means: the unit before the colon is dealt out evenly among doctors based on the units after it. Separate several rules with a semicolon.

Units ending in E or W are the old tower; units ending in N or S are the new tower; "ED" locations are the ED, which any doctor can cover.

3Review and export

Patients per doctor, by unit

In the output, the weekend doctor column becomes Final: it keeps the weekend doctor's name if that doctor is staying (even when Suggested gives the patient to someone else), and is blank for everyone else. Next to it, Suggested shows every patient's suggested doctor (staying doctors by name, new doctors as A, B, C…). "Copy Final and Suggested columns" gives both columns, one row per patient in the original order, ready to paste over the weekend doctor column in your named list. You can change any patient with the dropdown; highlighted rows have a different doctor than on the weekend.
